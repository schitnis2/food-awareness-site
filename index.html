Welcome to Food Safety Awareness

This site raises awareness about unsafe food ingredients often used in the U.S. that are banned in other countries.

Why is American Food Unhealthier?

While people around the world eat similar dishes, American versions often contain harmful additives and overly processed ingredients. This has made obesity and chronic illness more prevalent in the U.S.

  • Use of additives banned in other countries (e.g., Red 40, BHA, brominated oils)
  • High reliance on processed and fast food
  • Weaker food safety regulations
  • Marketing aimed at children and vulnerable populations
  • Portion sizes significantly larger than international norms
